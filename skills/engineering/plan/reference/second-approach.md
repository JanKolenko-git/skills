# A second approach from a seat

Read when Step 1's trigger holds or `panel` is set. A seat that never sees the arriving
candidate proposes an approach on its own, so Step 3 compares two approaches rather than
one and its shadow.

Print `1 agent: <model>, read-only, ≈ <50K + reads>K` first, `reads` the bytes of `goal`,
`criteria` and `constraints` divided by four. Then one `Agent` call per model,
`subagent_type: jankolenko-skills:panelist`, `model` set per call: by default the strongest
family other than the session's, because an alias in the session's own family resolves to
the session's model. With several models every seat gets the same brief, and two the same
is one model twice.

The brief: `goal`, `criteria`, `constraints`, `repo.path`, and the ask: one approach that
solves the goal, with its mechanism, the files it touches, what it assumes (each with a
cheap check), what breaks it later, and its cost in files outside the feature and shared
code changed. Withheld on purpose: `candidates`, your notes, the files you read.

Its approach enters Step 3 as one more entry, judged on the step's questions. The same
mechanism as the candidate is independent agreement, recorded as the deciding fact. A
different one that wins is the choice, or a Step 4 decision when the trade-off is the
user's. Check its assumptions as your own, and name the seat in `plan.approach`.

An `Agent` tool without `model`: seat it on the session's model, since the blindness is
the point. No `Agent` tool: write the second approach yourself and say so once.
